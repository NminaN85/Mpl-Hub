👷 My Profit Land – Beginner Guide (Worker Path)

Author: Mina Nashaat | Published: Feb 2026

If you are new to My Profit Land, the first thing you should do is read the Terms and Conditions carefully. This is a real-money game, and following the rules is extremely important to protect your account and your earnings.

If you are joining as an investor, you may skip this guide. However, reading it is still recommended, since understanding the worker system helps you better understand the game economy.

🔹 Step 1: Start Working

After completing registration and full verification:

  • Go to Activity
  • Click on Work
  • Press the Work button at the top of the company

Each work shift lasts 4 hours, and you can work up to 3 shifts per day.

📅 Beginner Strategy (First Days)

  • Work 2 shifts per day
  • After 3 days, receive the Government Work Bonus
  • Use the Homeless Shelter for 10% free energy

Important: Do NOT buy energy products, including mansions.

The increasing cost of energy will not be profitable in the early stage.

Exception: Every third day, just before receiving the government bonus, you may buy one Great Wine.

📊 Understanding Economic Score (ES)

  • Work twice per day → ES increases by +1
  • Work once per day → ES stays the same
  • No work → ES decreases by -1
  • Maximum ES is 100

🚀 When to Start Over time shifts?

After about a month, when your ES reaches 30–40 and you can log in more frequently, you may start considering overshifts.

After reaching 50 ES, overshifts are usually profitable.

Only buy enough energy to reach the 55% minimum requirement needed to activate overshifts.

⚖️ Final Advice

The worker path requires patience and discipline. Focus on steady ES growth, control your energy spending, and avoid unnecessary expenses.

Master the basics first — profit comes later.
