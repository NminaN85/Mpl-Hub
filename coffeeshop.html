Latest MPL Map Update

weapon Stacking * If you buy a weapon you already have equipped, it will now stack its durability instead of replacing it. You can stack up to 3x the base durability. You can only stack if your current weapon is below 70% of the max durability, so don't hoard too early.

* Buying a different quality weapon for the same slot will still replace the current one as before.

* Your weapons page now shows the current durability percentage plus how many backups you have stacked (e.g. +1, +2). Empty weapon slots now link directly to the market so you can buy what you need faster.

War Specialist+

* War Specialists can now upgrade to War Specialist+ using sVCT. The upgrade costs sVCT and lasts until your current War Specialist status expires.

* War Specialist+ benefits: beer cooldown goes from 3h to 1.5h, mocaccino cooldown from 5 min to 2.5 min, and your daily wine limit goes from 2 to 4.

* War Specialist+ players get a red crosshair icon next to their name so everyone knows who's serious.
